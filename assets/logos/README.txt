School logos for the spectator display (one PNG per school).

Filename = school name, lowercase, non-letters/numbers replaced by dashes. Examples:
  Quezon Science High School  ->  quezon-science-high-school.png
  St. Francis High School     ->  st-francis-high-school.png
  STI College Lucena, Inc.    ->  sti-college-lucena-inc.png

Use a square transparent PNG (500x500 or larger).
